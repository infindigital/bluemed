About Blue Diamond

Welcome to Blue Diamond Medical Staffing — your trusted partner in healthcare and education workforce solutions. In a nation facing unprecedented staffing shortages, we provide a lifeline for hospitals, nursing homes, rehab centers, clinics, and school districts.

Professionals across the country.
1000 professionals
Mission

To provide dependable staffing solutions that enhance care and education while creating growth opportunities for professionals.

Vision

To be a leading provider of trusted healthcare and education staffing across the U.S., known for quality, speed, and integrity.

Our network of highly trained, certified professionals are ready to deploy at a moment’s notice — when you need us most. With industry-leading fill rates, 24/7 client support, and competitive pricing, we are recognized for dependability in last-minute staffing emergencies and ongoing assignments.

Whether you’re a healthcare facility in need of licensed nurses or a school district seeking long-term substitute teachers, Blue Diamond delivers exceptional service and unmatched responsiveness. Let us solve your staffing challenges—fast, reliably, and professionally.
