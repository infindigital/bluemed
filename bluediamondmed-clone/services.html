Our Questions

Answers at Your Fingertips

Get quick and clear answers to your most common questions.
We’re here to help you understand our services and process with ease.

Can I apply for jobs if I am new to Missouri or Kansas?

Absolutely! We welcome licensed healthcare and education professionals from all backgrounds, including those relocating to Missouri or Kansas.

What does the CPR certification course include?

Our CPR courses cover Basic Life Support (BLS), Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), and First Aid, tailored for healthcare workers and educators with Missouri DESE compliance.

How do I request staffing for my facility or school?

Simply fill out the request form on our website with your institution details and contact information, and our team will connect you with the right professionals promptly.

How quickly can you fill a staffing request?

We pride ourselves on rapid response times and can often fill last-minute staffing needs within 24 to 48 hours, depending on the role and location.

Are your healthcare professionals fully licensed and certified?

Yes, all our medical staff—including RNs, LPNs, CNAs, and others—are fully licensed, credentialed, and undergo thorough background checks.

. What types of schools do you provide staffing for?

We staff public, private, and charter schools throughout Missouri and Kansas, offering certified teachers, substitutes, and classroom aides.
